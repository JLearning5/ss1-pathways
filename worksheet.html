SS1 Pathways classroom worksheet

What interests you?

For each question, tick one activity you’d most like to try. Leave it blank if you’re unsure.

  1. Which school project would you most like to try?

    • Grow two plants in different conditions and compare them.
    • Create a short play about life in your community.
    • Plan a small school market and set prices.

  2. What would you most like to find out about?

    • How people shape the rules we live by.
    • Why the price of food changes in the market.
    • How electricity reaches homes and schools.

  3. Which role would you choose for a group activity?

    • Keep track of what the group spends.
    • Test an idea and record what happens.
    • Interview people and tell their story.

  4. Which activity sounds most enjoyable?

    • Work out why a simple machine moves.
    • Compare products and decide which gives better value.
    • Discuss a character’s choices in a book or film.

  5. What would you most like to learn how to do?

    • Make a painting, song or story that shares an idea.
    • Use measurements to explain a change in nature.
    • Work out the costs of making and selling something.

  6. Which problem would you most like to work on?

    • Help a school club make the most of a small budget.
    • Help people understand both sides of a community debate.
    • Investigate which simple material keeps water cool longest.

Talk about your choices

Science often involves testing ideas and observing how things work. Arts often involves exploring ideas through stories, creative work or discussion. Commercial often involves making decisions about costs, trade and managing money.
